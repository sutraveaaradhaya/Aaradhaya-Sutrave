Daily Accessible Pill Container

Product design project focused on creating an accessible pill container for everyday use, organization, and interaction.

Overview

In this project, I led a team in designing and manufacturing a practical rotating pill container with accessibility and everyday usability as design requirements. The project was made for elderly with dementia or alzheimers. It's also just helpful for the elderly to organize the pills instead of having to remember when to have them or where they kept it. The upper container spins on a bearing fixed to the platform. When the magnet on the platform gets close to one of the pill container's magnets on the bottom, the pill container pops up. I organized the team into 4 system teams, organized team meetings, kept in check with deadlines as well as communicated with professor on progresses and feedback. At the end, our project was in the top 3 projects in the class.

Prototype in Action

A physical prototype was built to test the rotating mechanism, the magnet-triggered pop-up, and the overall day-to-day usability of the design.

Results

The final design demonstrated a practical approach to improving organization and accessibility.
