Selected research

01 / 05 · 2026 · RESEARCH · OPTICS

Microheater Optics Experiment

Investigated optical signals generated by a microheater using a lock-in amplifier and experimental instrumentation. Developed a LabVIEW data acquisition workflow for continuous measurement and MATLAB tools for analyzing the resulting experimental data.

02 / 05 · 2026 · RESEARCH · INSTRUMENTATION

Lock-In Amplifier Data Acquisition

Developed experimental data acquisition methods using an SRS lock-in amplifier, LabVIEW, and MATLAB to capture, process, and analyze weak measured signals from a waveform generator during laboratory research. Then compared the results such as peak amplitude and FFT to data from an oscilloscope.

03 / 05 · 2026 · RESEARCH · PROGRAMMING

Mesh Generation with Voronoi Tessellation

Developed MATLAB algorithms using Voronoi tessellation and half-edge data structures to generate and analyze computational meshes for finite element applications.

04 / 05 · 2026 · RESEARCH · ACOUSTICS

Textile Weave Structures in Kundt Tubes

Designed precision acoustic test apparatus components using SolidWorks and converted CAD designs into 3D-printed components for experimental testing.

05 / 05 · 2026 · CAPSTONE · ELECTRICAL

Backseat Minder

Designed and created a power system for a device that detects babies when left unattended in a car and when dangerous conditions are detected, it alerts the driver and EMS if left attended for longer.
